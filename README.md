# 🥏 KLONK! 

Prepare yourself for the ultimate adrenaline-fueled tabletop showdown! **KLONK!** takes the classic, high-octane thrill of air hockey and pushes it straight to the absolute limit. Fast-paced. Unforgiving. Pure physics-based chaos. 

Are you ready to make some noise?

## 🔥 Game Modes: Choose Your Battle

* **1v1 Local Showdown:** Go head-to-head against your best friend (or worst enemy) in a relentless, fast-finger battle for local bragging rights.
* **The Gauntlet (vs. Bots):** Not ready for human opponents? Test your reflexes against our built-in AI bots. They don't sweat, they don't sleep, and they only know one thing: how to score.
